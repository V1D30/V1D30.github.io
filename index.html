02 About

You don't have to be a developer to start building.

I'm V1D30. I have ideas, curiosity, and a willingness to try things—but I don't have a traditional coding background.

I use AI to help me understand the technical parts and turn my ideas into working projects. This is vibe coding: describing what I want, experimenting with the results, fixing what doesn't work, and learning as I go.

This site is an honest record of that journey. I'm not pretending to know all the code. I'm exploring what an ordinary person can create when curiosity meets AI.
